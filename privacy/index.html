Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

This policy explains how the private, owner-operated Whiskers & Mischief Pinterest publishing tool (the “Tool”) handles information. The Tool is used only by the owner of Whiskers & Mischief to prepare and publish selected content for the Whiskers & Mischief Etsy shop. It is not available to public users.

Nothing is published automatically. The owner reviews the finished content and specifically selects each individual Pin before publishing.

Information the Tool may access

When the owner connects a Pinterest business account through Pinterest’s official OAuth authorization process, the Tool may access only the information and permissions needed for its stated function:

  • Pinterest account identifiers and basic account information returned through OAuth;
  • board names and board identifiers so the owner can choose a destination board;
  • Pin information needed to create a selected Pin and confirm its status; and
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens required to maintain the authorized connection.

The Tool does not request or collect Pinterest passwords, browser cookies or session credentials. It does not scrape Pinterest.

How information is used

Pinterest information is used solely to authenticate the owner’s account, display eligible boards, publish Pins individually selected by the owner, and confirm the result. Public Etsy listing information and owner-approved creative assets are combined with Pinterest information only as necessary to provide that requested publishing function.

The Tool does not use Pinterest information for advertising profiles, cross-service tracking, competitor research, model training or unrelated analytics.

Human review and publishing controls

The Tool can prepare multiple local draft images and accompanying text, but draft preparation does not publish anything. Before any API publishing request, the owner can inspect the image, title, description, destination link and board, and must specifically choose each Pin to be published.

The Tool does not automatically follow or unfollow accounts, send messages, post comments, save third-party Pins or automate engagement.

Storage, security and retention

OAuth credentials are intended to be stored securely in owner-controlled private application storage and are not placed in public source code. Operational records are limited to what is reasonably needed to run the Tool, prevent duplicate publication, troubleshoot failures and document the owner’s publishing choices.

Pinterest account information is retained only while the connection remains authorized or while necessary to provide the requested function. It is not sold, rented or shared with data brokers.

Service providers

The Tool uses Pinterest’s official API for authorized Pinterest functions. ChatGPT Work may process owner instructions, draft content and creative assets when assisting with the private workflow. Etsy public listing information is used only to prepare the owner’s selected product content. GitHub Pages hosts this static information site and does not receive the Tool’s Pinterest access token through this site.

Each provider handles information under its own terms and privacy policy.

Revoking access and deletion

The owner can revoke the Tool’s Pinterest authorization through Pinterest account settings. After revocation, the Tool will no longer be able to make authorized Pinterest API requests. The owner may also request deletion of locally retained Pinterest account data and credentials using the contact method below.

Children’s privacy

The Tool is a private business utility and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the Tool’s operation or legal obligations change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact Whiskers & Mischief through the Whiskers & Mischief Etsy shop.
